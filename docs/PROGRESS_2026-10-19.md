## What Oct 19 settled

All numbers below are measured. The details are in `docs/final_phase_2026-10-17.md` 8.13-8.14 and
`docs/ALGORITHM.md` section 6.

- **Corrector accuracy is saturated at this data size.** None of these beat the shipped recipe: global or per-layer
  output scales, clipping, per-neuron loss weights, pooled end-to-end fine-tuning (forum 18255's objective), nine
  new v4 inputs, a root transform of the heavy-tailed inputs, or weight averaging (EMA). Seed noise is 0.15pp at 200
  networks.
- **The corrector's own cost was a lever.** The small corrector loses 0.14pp of held-out gain (-4.76% vs -4.90%) and
  bills 0.46% less, which won 0.20% on the grader.
- **Forum survey (all ~60 topics; four new write-ups since Oct 17).**
  - Young-atom pruning and dead-row schedules are already in our chain.
  - The "free" pair term is our null C0^3 test.
  - **E1** (kappa4 pendant edge, 18252) is a real gain here, -0.8% to -1.4% raw chain-only. But in our D21 form it
    needs one more dense product per layer, which costs about three times what it buys. Closed.
- **Grader facts:**
  - One zeroed network costs ~0.009 of mean score, and failures on the sealed 50 do not show on the board.
  - The same code can lose networks on a loaded day.
  - The private ranking runs fresh MLPs on 0.12.3.
  - Hence one nomination stays the 4-level twin.
