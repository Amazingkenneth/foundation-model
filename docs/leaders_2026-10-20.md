Question from the meeting: can we find the construction the top teams use, and use the last slots on it?
This note records the hypotheses, the measurements and the decision. Prototype code: `scripts/tchain/`.

## Facts that constrain the guess

- The networks are bias-free (`whestbench.simulation.run_mlp`: `x = relu(x @ w)`), so `h_L(c x) = c h_L(x)`. A
  common random scale of the activations is invisible to every mean (18248 s3.1).
- One 1024^3 product is 2^31 flops, so B is 1,024 products. C/B 0.12 is ~7.7 billed products per layer, about 15
  before Strassen. We run ~24 before Strassen, ~80% of them in the young kappa3 sources.

## Hypothesis 1: sampling with structured points (marius_binner's Phase 1 method). Measured: no.

marius_binner won Phase 1 with Kerdock / MUB spherical cubature. 8,192 forward passes cost exactly C/B 0.12, so
a point set at that size is the right budget. `scripts/tchain/cubtest.py` on mini m70 (1e9-sample labels, final
layer, label noise subtracted, 2 seeds):

| points | iid Gaussian | iid spherical | random orthonormal frames, antipodal | Hadamard x random signs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,048 | 2.99e-5 | 3.03e-5 | 1.95e-5 | 1.83e-5 |
| 8,192 | 7.32e-6 | 7.26e-6 | 4.51e-6 | 4.57e-6 |

Exact low-degree integration buys only 1.6x. At 4.5e-6 it is 400x short of 1.1e-8, so the leaders are analytic.

## Hypothesis 2: carried kappa4 core + K211 + scale-mode extraction (18248's "T-chain"). Built and measured.

il.miscusi (18248) report that ARC's AUGMENT chain (kappa4 carried as an r=1 matrix core G <- W G W^T) with
coherent scale-mode extraction is 0.58 of V29's error on the 100 public mini networks (~1.30e-8). That is the
leaders' raw band, and its shape (multiplicative, flat from layer 3) matches the leaders' public telemetry.
Extraction fits eps per layer from the post-ReLU (2,1) slice against `S_ij = v_i m_j + 2 m_i C_ij`, then removes
the mode from C (`(C - eps m m^T)/(1+eps)`), from kappa3 (`-2 eps Sym(C x m)`, two thin columns on the newborn's
legs) and from kappa4 (`G -= H[4 eps Pair3(C x C)]`). The three parts must move together. The gain needs ARC's
current AUGMENT term table (`get_all_terms_iso(k_max=3, d_max=4, augment=True)` on today's main; 504aldo's lean
calls it without `augment` and gets the small budget-2 table).

Our port is `scripts/tchain/estimator_c80.py` (cn71 chain, flags off = cn71 chain bit for bit):

| flag | what it does |
|---|---|
| `XAUG=1` | term program from ARC's current AUGMENT table (62 terms, new (3,1) output, `c_off^3` term; `aug_tables.py`) |
| `XG=1` | carried core: `G_pre = w^T G_post w`; use side `g4row = m diag G`, `wk431 = (m/2) G_off`; feed at birth from the exact core, stored source with `lambda_eff = <G_off, C_off>/<C_off, C_off>` |
| `XK211=1` (default with XG) | capped (2,1,1) production (19 terms of the augment table, the three kappa3 (1,1,1) terms skipped; `t211.py`) + the newborn's pK(1,1,1) -> K211 hub contraction (window 0, as in 18248's build) |
| `XEXT=f` | extraction at strength f (C, kappa3 thin columns, kappa4 through the same projection as production) |
| `XTK34=0` | learned k34 / MP steps off |

What this shows:
- The port reproduces the published behaviour: the per-layer eps (7.9e-4 at layer 0, 4e-4 at 9, 1.5e-4 at 14)
  matches 18248's medians (7.7e-4, 2.4e-4 at 8, 1.4e-4 at 14); without extraction the carried core runs away.
- In our engine it lands at the level of our chain without its learned operators (+14% vs the base on 8 nets),
  and the learned operators (worth 11-13% here) cannot be combined with it: they were trained on the lambda
  chain and blow up on the extracted state.
- Extraction does not make old kappa3 cheap here: joining the shared basis at age 3 hurts the T-chain more than
  the base. So the hoped-for cost lever (fewer dense young sources) does not appear.
- Its bill is higher than ours: +2 dense products per layer for the core transport, plus the capped production
  and the newborn contraction (18248 booked 77 units for the loop in their engine).

18248 reached the same end point in e30_a's engine: their T-chain build graded 0.854 of their best raw error at
twice the bill and lost on score.

### The same construction without truncation (504aldo's lean vehicle, patched to ARC's current rule)

A helper run patched `lean_k3_aug.py` to parity with ARC main 93d091a factored AUGMENT (current term table with
`augment=True` plus ARC's factored filter, and ARC's current 3-pair birth rule; parity at n=48 to 1e-14), added the
extraction exactly (the kappa3 mode as an extra hub pair, the kappa4 part through the projection including the
2 C_b^2 part), and ran float64, untruncated, on the same 8 networks (`/tmp/claude-0/lean_ext/RESULTS.md`):

| lean variant | m70-m77 mean final MSE | vs cn71 (with corrector, 1.844e-8) |
|---|---|---|
| AUGMENT, no extraction | 1.81e-8 | 0.98 (4/8) |
| **AUGMENT + extraction 0.5** | **1.29e-8** | **0.70 (8/8)** |
| + no K211 (G from K4, K22, K31) | 2.45e-8 (2 nets) | 1.40 |
| + K211 from the newest source only (504aldo's flag, inconsistent: 'a' part without its 'b' part) | 29.6e-8 (1 net) | diverges |
| + consistent K211 window: newest old source + births | 1.34e-8 (4 nets) | 0.72 (4/4) |
| + consistent K211 window 0: births only (18248's production setting) | 1.33e-8 (3 nets) | 0.79 (3/3) |
| + budget-2 term table (`augment=False`) instead of the AUGMENT table | 3.49e-8 | 1.89 |
| + kappa3 window of 3 / 5 sources (old sources dropped) | 38.2e-8 / 15.7e-8 (4 nets) | diverges |
| lean SIMPLE (current rule), for reference | 4.10e-8 | 2.22 |

Per-layer gain of extraction (B/A): 0.95 at layer 1, 0.81 at 3, 0.77 at 7, 0.74 at 11, 0.71 at 15, the
all-layer multiplicative shape 18248 and the e30_a write-up read off the leaders' telemetry.

So the construction is worth -21% to -30% against our best build, corrector included. Window-0 K211 (births
only) keeps most of it, so the K211 cost is not the barrier. What it needs is the AUGMENT term table and all
old kappa3 sources (dropping them diverges).

Our engine port uses window-0 K211 too, yet it is +14% vs cn71's chain instead of ~0.75. The gap (~1.7x vs the
lean) must then come from what differs between the two chains:
- our truncations: tiers 320/160, residual rank 6, feedback rank 2, the dead row/column masks. 18248 measured V29's
  truncations at +17% in the lean, and found e30_a's dead-neuron removal corrupted the slices the kappa4
  production reads.
- our V1.6 births vs ARC's current 3-pair rule (+7% in 18248's lean).
- ARC's factored filter (`factored_keeps_term`), which our port's table generator does not apply.
- the leading-Wick approximation of C_b in our kappa3 mode columns.

**Layer-by-layer comparison (Oct 20, 22:40-23:20 UTC, m70).** Both chains dumped per layer, carried core with
window-0 K211 and no extraction, the lean with 504aldo's birth rule:
- Layer 0 matches to float32 precision: the AUGMENT term table (`aug_tables.py`, no factored filter needed at
  layer 0), K(4,), K(2,2), K(3,1), the capped (2,1,1) production, the newborn contraction and G (all ~5e-7 rel).
- From layer 1 G_off diverges (13%, then 35% at layer 2) while its slice inputs still agree to <= 1e-3, i.e. the
  difference sits in what the newborn sources carry, not in the formulas.
- Walking the lean toward our engine (window-0 K211, extraction 0.5, final MSE on m70):

| lean configuration | m70 |
|---|---|
| ARC current births | 1.331e-8 |
| + 504aldo's birth rule | 1.423e-8 (+7%) |
| + B2 hub pair and WK22 term dropped (our V1.6 ablations) | 1.454e-8 (+2%) |
| + D21 feedback rank 2 (ours) | 1.697e-8 (+17%) |
| + residual rank 6 only (ours) | 1.486e-8 (+2%) |
| + feedback 2 and residual 6 (both ours) | 1.717e-8 (+18%) |
| + feedback 16, residual 32 | 1.596e-8 (+10%) |

  Our tiers and dead-row/column masks change our port by <= 2% (no-confine 1.88e-8, no masks 1.88e-8 vs 1.915e-8).
  So the main culprit is the rank-2 D21 feedback leg, which the lambda chain tolerates (rank 2 vs 16 was "chaos
  level" there) but the carried-core chain does not.
- Our port with feedback rank 64 and residual rank 32 (extraction 0.25): m70 1.649e-8 (-9% vs the base chain;
  the base with the same ranks: -0.8%), but on 8 nets **1.969e-8, +1.7% vs the base chain (3/8 better)**.
- Bill (0.12.3 in-process, seed 1): cn71 0.191; the prototype at ranks 2/6 **0.331**, at ranks 64/32 **0.400**.
  An optimised core loop (Strassen G transport, capped production merged into ~2 products) is still ~+0.05, and
  the rank-64 feedback legs ~+0.07, so ~0.28 at chain-only parity. Closed.

Closing the rest of the gap would need ARC's current birth rule and full-rank feedback, i.e. more dense legs. Even if it is closed, the core loop costs
~6 products per layer: G transport, the capped production merged into ~2 products, and the newborn contraction.
That takes C/B 0.19 -> ~0.24. At the truncated lean's level (~1.5e-8 chain-only) that scores ~3.6e-9 before a
corrector, about cn71's 3.32e-9. So it would pay only together with a cheaper kappa3 engine, which the leaders
evidently have and we do not.

## Decision

No build from this line before the deadline. To pay, it needs (1) k34 / MP retrained on the extracted chain,
(2) the corrector retrained on its dumps, (3) the port's ~1.7x gap to the lean closed (truncations, births,
ARC's factored filter; see above), and (4) cost engineering of the core loop and a cheaper kappa3 engine. That is several days. cn71 (337265) + cn71r (337275) stay the nominations.

What the leaders most likely run, in our reading: an AUGMENT-family chain with a carried kappa4 core and the scale
mode removed, with learned pieces trained on that chain rather than on a lambda chain, and a cheaper kappa3 engine
than V29's hub sources. We have the first part working, but not the other two.
