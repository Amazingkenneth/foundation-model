## Facts that constrain the guess

- The networks are bias-free (`whestbench.simulation.run_mlp`: `x = relu(x @ w)`), so `h_L(c x) = c h_L(x)`. A
  common random scale of the activations is invisible to every mean (18248 s3.1).
- One 1024^3 product is 2^31 flops, so B is 1,024 products. C/B 0.12 is ~7.7 billed products per layer, about 15
  before Strassen. We run ~24 before Strassen, ~80% of them in the young kappa3 sources.

## Hypothesis 1: sampling with structured points (marius_binner's Phase 1 method). Measured: no.

marius_binner won Phase 1 with Kerdock / MUB spherical cubature. 8,192 forward passes cost exactly C/B 0.12, so
a point set at that size is the right budget. `scripts/tchain/cubtest.py` on mini m70 (1e9-sample labels, final
layer, label noise subtracted, 2 seeds):

| points | iid Gaussian | iid spherical | random orthonormal frames, antipodal | Hadamard x random signs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2,048 | 2.99e-5 | 3.03e-5 | 1.95e-5 | 1.83e-5 |
| 8,192 | 7.32e-6 | 7.26e-6 | 4.51e-6 | 4.57e-6 |

Exact low-degree integration buys only 1.6x. At 4.5e-6 it is 400x short of 1.1e-8, so the leaders are analytic.

## Hypothesis 2: carried kappa4 core + K211 + scale-mode extraction (18248's "T-chain"). Built and measured.

il.miscusi (18248) report that ARC's AUGMENT chain (kappa4 carried as an r=1 matrix core G <- W G W^T) with
coherent scale-mode extraction is 0.58 of V29's error on the 100 public mini networks (~1.30e-8). That is the
leaders' raw band, and its shape (multiplicative, flat from layer 3) matches the leaders' public telemetry.
Extraction fits eps per layer from the post-ReLU (2,1) slice against `S_ij = v_i m_j + 2 m_i C_ij`, then removes
the mode from C (`(C - eps m m^T)/(1+eps)`), from kappa3 (`-2 eps Sym(C x m)`, two thin columns on the newborn's
legs) and from kappa4 (`G -= H[4 eps Pair3(C x C)]`). The three parts must move together. The gain needs ARC's
current AUGMENT term table (`get_all_terms_iso(k_max=3, d_max=4, augment=True)` on today's main; 504aldo's lean
calls it without `augment` and gets the small budget-2 table).

Our port is `scripts/tchain/estimator_c80.py` (cn71 chain, flags off = cn71 chain bit for bit):

| flag | what it does |
|---|---|
| `XAUG=1` | term program from ARC's current AUGMENT table (62 terms, new (3,1) output, `c_off^3` term; `aug_tables.py`) |
| `XG=1` | carried core: `G_pre = w^T G_post w`; use side `g4row = m diag G`, `wk431 = (m/2) G_off`; feed at birth from the exact core, stored source with `lambda_eff = <G_off, C_off>/<C_off, C_off>` |
| `XK211=1` (default with XG) | capped (2,1,1) production (19 terms of the augment table, the three kappa3 (1,1,1) terms skipped; `t211.py`) + the newborn's pK(1,1,1) -> K211 hub contraction (window 0, as in 18248's build) |
| `XEXT=f` | extraction at strength f (C, kappa3 thin columns, kappa4 through the same projection as production) |
| `XTK34=0` | learned k34 / MP steps off |

What this shows:
- The port reproduces the published behaviour: the per-layer eps (7.9e-4 at layer 0, 4e-4 at 9, 1.5e-4 at 14)
  matches 18248's medians (7.7e-4, 2.4e-4 at 8, 1.4e-4 at 14); without extraction the carried core runs away.
- In our engine it lands at the level of our chain without its learned operators (+14% vs the base on 8 nets),
  and the learned operators (worth 11-13% here) cannot be combined with it: they were trained on the lambda
  chain and blow up on the extracted state.
- Extraction does not make old kappa3 cheap here: joining the shared basis at age 3 hurts the T-chain more than
  the base. So the hoped-for cost lever (fewer dense young sources) does not appear.
- Its bill is higher than ours: +2 dense products per layer for the core transport, plus the capped production
  and the newborn contraction (18248 booked 77 units for the loop in their engine).

18248 reached the same end point in e30_a's engine: their T-chain build graded 0.854 of their best raw error at
twice the bill and lost on score.

## Decision

No build from this line before the deadline. To pay, it needs (1) k34 / MP retrained on the extracted chain,
(2) the corrector retrained on its dumps, (3) the K211 old-source window and the skipped (1,1,1) terms, and
(4) cost engineering of the core loop. That is several days. cn71 (337265) + cn71r (337275) stay the nominations.

What the leaders most likely run, in our reading: an AUGMENT-family chain with a carried kappa4 core and the scale
mode removed, with learned pieces trained on that chain rather than on a lambda chain, and a cheaper kappa3 engine
than V29's hub sources. We have the first part working, but not the other two.
